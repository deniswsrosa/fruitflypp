FruitFly++

Based on the real brain of a fruit fly.

The wiring comes from the real MaleCNS connectome: 19,463 neurons and 340,922 anatomical connections. A neural simulation follows this wiring as the fly watches the keyboard light up. The SQL++ queries use templates and run for real against Couchbase's travel-sample.

IMPORTANT: The fruitfly is not a DBA.

MaleCNS v1.0 · visual-to-motor circuit · drag to rotate, scroll to zoom
REAL CONNECTOME · SIMULATED ACTIVITY — 19,463 neurons, 340,922 anatomical connections. The trained connectome rate network responds to illuminated keys and the colored target through a compound-eye model. Red = firing above rest; blue = below rest. SQL++ queries are templates executed against travel-sample.
